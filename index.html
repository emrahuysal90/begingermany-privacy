  • We do not use advertising or analytics (tracking) tools.
  • Document photos and PDFs you attach are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • To send notifications, only a device notification token together with your chosen city, language and residence type is sent to the server.

3. Data stored only on your device

  • Your profile choices: language, residence/visa type, city
  • Your task progress and checked documents
  • Your arrival date in Germany and German level preference (Milestones screen)
  • Photos and PDF files you attach to documents

This data is never sent to us or to third parties. You can delete all of it via Settings → Delete All Data or by uninstalling the app.
